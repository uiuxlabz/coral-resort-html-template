About Coraluxe

Born from a Love of the Sea

Coraluxe was founded with a singular vision: to create a resort where luxury is not an intrusion on nature, but a conversation with it. Every detail — from the orientation of our villas to the materials we source — is guided by the rhythms of the ocean.

We are located in Baa Atoll, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, one of the most biodiverse marine environments on Earth. Our responsibility is not just to offer exceptional hospitality, but to protect the reef and the communities that depend on it.

“We do not build on the ocean. We build with it. Every pillar, every terrace, every meal is shaped by the tides and the light that changes them.”

From our coral restoration programme to our zero-waste kitchen, sustainability is not a policy at Coraluxe — it is our architecture.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Reef Conservation

Every stay contributes to our coral propagation programme. We have planted over 12,000 coral fragments since 2018.

Community First

We partner with local island communities for employment, supplies, and cultural exchange — because luxury should uplift, not displace.

Zero-Waste Kitchen

Our chefs work with local fishermen and island farmers. Nothing is wasted — every ingredient tells a story of the atoll.

Leadership

Ayesha Naseer

General Manager

Ayesha has spent two decades in luxury hospitality across the Indian Ocean. She joined Coraluxe in 2019 with a mission to redefine what a Maldivian resort can be — more intimate, more rooted, more alive.

Under her leadership, Coraluxe has earned recognition for its marine conservation work, its community partnerships, and a guest experience that feels less like a hotel and more like a home.

“The best resorts I have visited were the ones that made me forget I was a guest. That is what we aspire to every single day.”
