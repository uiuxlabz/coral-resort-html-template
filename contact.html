Getting Here

Your Journey to Coraluxe

Baa Atoll is a 35-minute seaplane flight from Malé International Airport, with breathtaking aerial views of the atoll chain.

By Seaplane

35-minute scenic flight from Malé International Airport (MLE). Transfers arranged by our team upon booking confirmation.

By Speedboat

Domestic flight to Dharavandhoo Airport (DRV), followed by a 10-minute speedboat ride directly to the resort.

Visa & Entry

Free 30-day tourist visa on arrival for all nationalities. Our team handles all immigration formalities upon landing.
